Privacy Policy

Your health data privacy and security are our top priorities. This policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal health information.

Last Updated: October 4, 2025

Important Notice

By using the Personal Health Tracker, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our services.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

Name, email address, age, gender, contact information, and medical history you provide during registration.

Health Data

Blood pressure readings, blood sugar levels, heart rate, weight measurements, symptoms, and other health vitals you log in the application.

Usage Information

Login times, features accessed, pages viewed, and interaction patterns within the application.

Doctor Communications

Medical notes, recommendations, and advice provided by healthcare professionals through the platform.

How We Use Your Information

Health Tracking & Visualization

We process your health data to create personalized charts, graphs, and trend analysis to help you monitor your wellness over time.

AI-Powered Recommendations

Your health vitals are analyzed by our AI models to generate personalized health insights, risk predictions, and wellness recommendations. These are informational only and not medical diagnoses.

Doctor-Patient Care

Your assigned healthcare providers can access your health logs to provide informed medical advice, track your progress, and add treatment notes.

Service Improvement

We use aggregated, anonymized data to improve our AI models, enhance platform features, and conduct medical research (only with your explicit consent).

Health Alerts

We may send you notifications about unusual health patterns, appointment reminders, or important platform updates.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

Healthcare Providers

Your health information is shared only with doctors you explicitly assign or authorize within the platform. Doctors can view your logs, trends, and add medical notes.

No Third-Party Sales

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal health information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Service Providers

We may share data with trusted service providers (cloud hosting, analytics) who assist in operating our platform, under strict confidentiality agreements.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the platform.

Research (Opt-In Only)

Anonymized health data may be used for medical research or AI model improvement only if you provide explicit consent through your account settings.

Health Data Compliance

HIPAA Compliance (US)

We implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your Protected Health Information (PHI) in accordance with HIPAA standards.

GDPR Compliance (EU)

For European users, we comply with GDPR requirements including data minimization, purpose limitation, and your rights to access, rectify, and erase your data.

Data Encryption

All health data is encrypted both in transit (using TLS/SSL) and at rest (using AES-256 encryption) to prevent unauthorized access.

Regular Audits

We conduct regular security assessments, penetration testing, and compliance audits to ensure the highest protection standards.

Your Rights & Controls

Access Your Data

You can view, download, and review all your health information at any time through your account dashboard.

Correct Information

You have the right to update or correct any inaccurate personal or health information in your profile.

Delete Your Data

You may request deletion of your account and all associated health data. Some data may be retained for legal compliance purposes as specified by law.

Export Health Records

Download your complete health history in a portable format (CSV/PDF) for personal records or transfer to another provider.

Manage Doctor Access

Control which healthcare providers can view your health data. You can revoke doctor access at any time.

Opt-Out of AI Analysis

You may disable AI-powered recommendations in your settings, though this will limit certain platform features.

Data Security Measures

Encryption Standards

All data transmissions use SSL/TLS encryption. Stored data is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

Secure Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA), secure password policies, and session management protect your account from unauthorized access.

Access Controls

Role-based access controls ensure that only authorized personnel (doctors, admins) can access specific data based on their role.

Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify you within 72 hours and provide details about the incident and remediation steps.

Server Security

Our servers are hosted in secure, HIPAA-compliant data centers with physical access controls, redundancy, and disaster recovery measures.

Data Retention

Active Accounts

Health logs and personal data are retained as long as your account remains active to provide continuous health tracking services.

Account Deletion

Upon account deletion, most personal data is removed within 30 days. Some records may be retained for up to 7 years to comply with medical record-keeping laws.

Legal Obligations

Certain data may be retained longer if required by law, regulation, or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention, dispute resolution).

Cookies & Tracking

Essential Cookies

We use necessary cookies for authentication, session management, and basic platform functionality. These cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

Optional analytics cookies help us understand how users interact with the platform to improve user experience. You can opt out in your browser settings.

No Advertising Cookies

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising or share cookie data with advertising networks.

Third-Party Services

Our platform may use the following categories of third-party services:

  • Cloud Hosting: For secure data storage and application hosting (HIPAA-compliant providers)
  • AI Model Providers: For health risk predictions and recommendations (data is anonymized)
  • Analytics Tools: To understand platform usage and improve user experience
  • Email Services: For account notifications, alerts, and communication

All third-party service providers are required to maintain strict confidentiality and security standards.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • For significant changes, we will notify you via email or in-app notification
  • Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy
